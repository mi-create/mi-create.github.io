PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Takara Nihongo Kanji (hereinafter referred to as the "App") collects, uses, and protects your information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information Collection and Storage

  • Local Data Only: The App is designed to operate completely offline. All user data, including learning history, preferences, and progress, is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, upload, or store your personal learning data on any remote servers.
  • Personal Information: We do not require you to create an account or provide personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the core features of the App.

2. Use of Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App may use third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob, to display ads (including banner and rewarded ads) to support the development and maintenance of the App.

  • Ad Networks: Google AdMob may use device identifiers, advertising IDs, and technical data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

3. External Links and Support

The App includes links to external platforms such as Zalo and Google Forms for user support, lesson consultations, and bug reporting. If you choose to use these external services, your interactions will be governed by the respective privacy policies of those third-party platforms.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences learning Japanese Kanji and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this page with an updated revision date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the feedback link (Google Form).
